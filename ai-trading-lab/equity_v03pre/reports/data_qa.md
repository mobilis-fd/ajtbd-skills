## 5. Prices vs second source (Nasdaq historical API, 2016-11-25 … 2026-11-06 only)

Stooq (the README-suggested alternative) refused automated downloads from both the cloud host (connection reset) and a browser ("Access denied"); not bypassed. Nasdaq provides only the last 10 years, so T3 (2003–2016) has no second price source.

Method: per calendar month, compare session count, sum of day numbers, sum of open and close in cents. Months with any difference were then compared day by day (`nasdaq_days_mismatch_months.csv`).
